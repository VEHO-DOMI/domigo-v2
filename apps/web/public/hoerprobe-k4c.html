Text mitlesen (erst NACH dem Hören) 
Last Tuesday was not a good day for me. Well, the morning was very good.
Our class was at the museum at nine o'clock. There was a new exhibition of modern art, and everybody wanted to look at it. Our teacher, Mrs Smith, had the tickets. The admission fee was three pounds for children. I also bought a picture book for five pounds. That was quite expensive, but the pictures in it were awesome.
"Leonie, come and look at this!" Mrs Smith called. She was in the first room, next to a red glass box.
The rooms were big and the art was very modern. In the second room there was a white plate on the wall, and everybody asked why. In the third room there was a black sculpture of a horse, and it was my favourite, because it was so big.
Then I looked at a chair near the door. There was a brown jacket on the chair. The jacket was dirty, and it was such a funny thing in a museum. Was it art? Was it worth a lot of money? I didn't know. So I called my best friend David.
I asked him about the funny thing near the door, and David laughed and laughed. He wanted to know if it was dirty. It was very dirty, and there was no price next to it. And then David knew, and I knew it too.
It wasn't a sculpture at all. It was the jacket of the cleaner. She was in the third room, and her jacket was on the chair. The jacket and the chair were not part of the exhibition.
Then Mrs Smith was next to me. "What's the matter?" she asked. I didn't tell her, because I was so embarrassed.
We were back at school at two o'clock. In the afternoon my mum asked me about the museum, and I talked about the horse and about the picture book. I didn't tell her about the jacket. Now you know it too. It is my secret, so please don't pass it on!
