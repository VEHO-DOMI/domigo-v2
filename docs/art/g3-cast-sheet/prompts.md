```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset: one character reference portrait for the FOURTEEN cast sheet. Semi-realistic European graphic novel illustration. A contemporary Austrian school world, unless the scene names somewhere else. All characters aged 12-15 must look their real age.
BEN — a 13-year-old boy, average height, the warmest of the group. SANDY-BLOND wavy hair, thick and tousled, casually messy and never styled. A few FRECKLES across the bridge of his nose. Lean build, relaxed posture. BRIGHT ORANGE quilted PUFFER VEST (colour #FF6B35, a solid single orange — never striped, rainbow or patterned) worn over graphic tees or plain bright colours. Jeans and worn white trainers. Warm brown eyes, round open face, easy grin showing a small gap between his two front teeth. He looks like a regular teenager — friendly, approachable, not trying too hard.
One single character only, neutral warm off-white paper background. Vertical portrait from crown to upper thighs with generous headroom, both shoulders and the complete orange vest visible. Relaxed friendly standing pose, arms comfortably down, small natural gap-toothed smile. Soft even painted daylight. No lettering or captions, no decorative frame. This is the neutral identity reference for later story scenes. Do not depict or name any real person — no actors, musicians, athletes or other public figures, and no recognisable likeness; describe an impression instead if one must be evoked. Every person shown is a fictional character described above. No watermarks. THIS IS A DRAWING, NOT A PHOTOGRAPH. No photorealism, no camera depth-of-field or bokeh, no lens flare, no film grain, no HDR, no cinematic colour grading, no 3D render.
```
